Core Idea

Selling tokens is selling electricity

The grid does not make money from selling meters, but from toll fees — so do we: every AI inference, every roast, every warehouse receipt is a measurable capability call.

⚡

Capability packaging

Roasting capacity, warehousing attestation, store space, AI compute, farming plans — packaged as standardized service bundles.

🎟️

Tokenization

Each bundle maps to measurable, tradable, incentivizable token rights: roast packs, receipts, wisdom packs, carbon credits.

🏭

Capability fees

When tokens are consumed, real resources are invoked, and the platform charges a service fee per flow — like the grid charges a toll.
