Franchise models

Two franchise models

Recommended

Model A: Zero-cash franchise

  • Performance deposit: 30,000 CNY (fully refundable)
  • Tech service fee: 5% of monthly revenue (JBP settlement)
  • Equipment deposit: 10,000 CNY (leased smart equipment)
  • Contract term: 3 years
Standard

Model B: Standard franchise

  • One-time franchise fee: 100,000 CNY
  • Tech service fee: 2% of monthly revenue (cash)
  • Equipment purchase: ~150,000 CNY (incl. smart devices)
  • Revenue share: 100% of revenue goes to the franchisee

💰 Revenue: 100% of store revenue goes to the franchisee, minus supply-chain purchases, delivery commissions and tech service fees (JBP). Every fee is auditable in the Owner app.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the deposit refundable?
On contract expiry without breach, fully refunded without interest.
How are AI calls billed?
Metered by actual usage, then priced: in-plan calls are covered by the monthly fee; beyond that, B-side at a 50 JBP floor and cost × 5.
Who bears consumer refunds?
Product refunds follow platform policy; JBP top-up refunds go through the 7-day cooling-off process.
Can JBP be cashed out?
No. JBP is for in-platform use only; off-exchange trading or withdrawal is a breach.
What do I get from reporting data?
500 JBP/month for full reporting; data makes Jebi Brain understand your store — accuracy becomes incremental sales.
